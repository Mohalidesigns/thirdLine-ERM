01What the analysis found

Three findings drive the entire plan.

Corporater's moat is architecture, not features

They sell a metadata-driven object platform with 250+ configurable business objects and ship ~30 "solutions" that are configuration packages over one engine. A digital twin of the organisation, a period-aware measure model, context-bound widgets, and configure-don't-code. Copying the feature list produces a checklist product. Copying the architecture makes every subsequent module cheap.

We already own things they don't

Schema-level Nigerian regulatory depth no international vendor ships — CBN, NFIU STR/CTR, BOFIA, NDIC, EFCC reportability with real deadlines and statutory citations; Basel L1/L2/L3 plus CBN ORMS dual taxonomy; five-way recovery decomposition; a working Monte Carlo engine with VaR at four confidence levels and a correctly structured ICAAP model. Most mid-market GRC tools have nothing in the quantification layer.

The environment is the durable moat

Data residency became a gating requirement on 15 June 2026 — all Nigerian payment data in Nigeria by 1 January 2027. Three dated regulatory waves created simultaneous demand. No established Nigerian-built ERM platform exists. Global vendors will copy a content library; they will not rebuild their architecture for 2G connectivity and daily power cuts, and they will not price in naira.

06Where we win

Attack Corporater's real gaps

  • Quantification depth — they stop at Monte Carlo; no public evidence of FAIR, loss exceedance curves, LDA fitting or capital modelling
  • Continuous control monitoring — immature everywhere; Forrester says this is where the market is heading
  • Connector marketplace — their ETL is pull-only with no click-to-connect library
  • Third-party risk depth — no tiering models, contract lifecycle or nth-party mapping
  • Time-to-value — "more configuration options than any other software" is also the classic Archer trap

Own what no global vendor will build

  • Offline-first architecture — a full RCSA completed with the radio off, synced without loss
  • USSD and WhatsApp capture — the only way to get real event data out of a 40,000-agent network
  • Inflation-indexed thresholds — appetite defined as a formula, re-baselined annually with board approval. Fixes the "everything is High" drift
  • Nigeria-resident deployment — an exclusion criterion for every payments-adjacent buyer from 1 January 2027
  • Pre-loaded regulatory content — CBN, SEC, NAICOM, PenCom, NDPC and FRC libraries. Global vendors cannot economically maintain these
  • Naira flat-rate pricing — per-seat pricing structurally kills the front-line capture that makes the product valuable
